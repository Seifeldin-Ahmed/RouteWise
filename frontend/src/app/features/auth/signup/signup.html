Four steps stand
between you and paid.

RouteWise runs them on every payment you send, then records what it did. Nothing to configure once you are in.

  1. Open right now Inside the gateway's operating days and hours, midnight-crossing windows included.
  2. Allowance left Your daily quota on that gateway covers the whole payment, every chunk of it.
  3. Takes the amount At or above its minimum. Over its ceiling it is offered as a split instead.
  4. Wins the ranking Cheapest then fastest, or fastest then cheapest. Ties break on the lowest id.
