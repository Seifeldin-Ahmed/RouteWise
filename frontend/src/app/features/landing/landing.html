Four filters, then a ranking

A gateway that fails any one of these is never offered. Only what clears all four ever reaches the payment.

  1. 01

    Open right now

    Inside its operating days and its hours, including windows that run past midnight.

  2. 02

    Allowance left

    Your daily quota on that gateway still covers the whole payment, every chunk included.

  3. 03

    Takes the amount

    At or above its minimum. Over its ceiling it is offered as a split instead.

  4. 04

    Wins the ranking

    Cheapest then fastest, or fastest then cheapest. Ties break on the lowest gateway id.

Too big for one transaction? It still goes through.

RouteWise asks for the fewest chunks that work, then rebalances so no chunk falls under the gateway's minimum - the leftover gets topped up out of the one before it. Take 25,100 EGP through a gateway that caps at 25,000: a bare remainder of 100 would have been under its 2,000 minimum, so the split moves to 23,100 and 2,000.
